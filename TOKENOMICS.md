# $NVAI Utility & Year 1 Burn Plan

## Status

Planned product/token utility design. $NVAI is not currently integrated into NOVA checkout, and no burn has occurred.

## Subscription utility

- NOVA subscription floor: **$9/month**.
- Planned $NVAI payment utility discount: **57%**.
- At the $9 floor, the planned $NVAI equivalent is **$3.87**.
- The discount is a product utility parameter, not a promise of token value or investment return.

## Year 1 buyback & burn

For the first twelve consecutive months after the $NVAI payment/buyback program is officially activated:

1. Measure subscription revenue actually received through $NVAI payments for the month.
2. Apply the predefined revenue allocation percentage to that eligible revenue.
3. Use the resulting amount for the scheduled monthly $NVAI buyback.
4. Permanently burn **100% of the $NVAI acquired by that buyback**.
5. Record and publish the month number, eligible revenue, allocation amount, amount purchased, and burn transaction reference where technically available.

### Burn limit

- **Exactly 12 scheduled monthly burn events** are planned for Year 1.
- After the 12th event, this automatic program ends.
- No perpetual automatic burn is promised by this plan.
- Any successor mechanism would need a separately documented product, governance, technical, and compliance decision.

## Not yet fixed

- Total $NVAI supply
- Revenue allocation percentage for buyback
- Token launch chain and deployment structure
- Treasury/ecosystem allocations
- Vesting and lockups
- Payment processor/on-chain settlement
- Geographic availability and compliance controls

## Design principle

**$NVAI utility payment → subscription revenue → Year 1 monthly buyback → $NVAI burn**

This is not a guarantee of price appreciation, profit, liquidity, or investment return.

## Implementation rule

Until real token payments, accounting, buyback execution, and burn verification are implemented on the backend, the website must label the mechanism as **planned** rather than active.
