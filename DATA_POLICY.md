# NOVA Dataset & Model Provenance Policy

NOVA native training data must have a recorded provenance entry before it is used.

For every source, record:
- source name
- creator/owner
- license or permission
- URL or acquisition record
- date acquired
- permitted use
- attribution requirement
- whether commercial training is permitted

Do not place a video into the training dataset merely because it is publicly viewable.

Do not add:
- proprietary model checkpoints,
- proprietary API outputs for the purpose of training a competing model,
- scraped copyrighted collections without documented permission,
- assets whose license forbids machine-learning training.

The goal is to make the NOVA dataset auditable from the beginning.
